# SMA local epoxy keyword strategy

Research date: 11 September 2026. Scope: Mississauga, Oakville, Toronto, Burlington and Milton, Ontario. Implementation stays local; this document authorizes no deployment or account changes.

Prioritize people looking for a local installer, then answer the questions that stop them requesting an estimate: cost, system choice, preparation, timing and suitability. These are qualitative priorities based on buying intent, SMA's stated services and the pages inspected below. They are not a claim that any phrase has the highest search volume or lowest competition. Keyword Planner, Search Console and paid keyword-volume/difficulty data were unavailable. No search volumes, keyword difficulty scores, current positions or projected rankings are supplied. No Google People Also Ask extraction was performed.

## Priorities and page ownership

`P1` means direct service-selection or estimate intent. `P2` means a practical question that helps someone assess a project or remove an objection. These labels rank editorial attention, not search volume. The companion [keyword map](seo-keyword-map.csv) contains 48 phrases with one primary target each.

| General epoxy questions | `/epoxy-flooring-questions/` answer hub | Get a short answer and choose the detailed guide. |
| Cost, comparison and contractor choice | Dedicated guides listed below | Compare proposals on scope and evidence. |
| Timing, preparation, care and problems | Dedicated guides listed below | Plan access, understand limitations, ask useful questions. |

Maintain one city page per city. Do not generate service-by-city combination pages or near-identical neighbourhood pages to cover every keyword permutation. A service page owns the service explanation; a city page owns coverage and useful local project logistics. Use natural variants in relevant titles, headings and copy without repeating every CSV phrase. “Near me” is a local-intent concept, not a reason to add a fake location or awkward text.

The answer hub should summarize these decisions and link to their guides. Each guide should answer its question early, explain the conditions that change the answer and offer a relevant service/estimate link. Use manufacturer documentation for technical claims and make the distinction between general guidance and SMA's confirmed practices explicit. This follows Google's emphasis on useful, reliable content created for people. [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

Keep FAQs for readers. Google's documentation records that FAQ rich results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026 and their documentation was removed in June. Do not promise FAQ rich results or treat FAQ markup as the objective. [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

## Evidence that can help a customer choose SMA

The owner confirmed on 11 September 2026 that SMA prefers polyaspartic for the customer results it aims to deliver, values efficient work with less household disruption, and offers a five-year warranty. These are approved business statements and now support the home page, service and city pages, guides and answer hub. Describe the polyaspartic preference as SMA's approach; it does not establish that every polyaspartic system is superior on every surface. The speed preference does not establish a fixed installation or return-to-use time.

Warranty duration is confirmed; coverage, exclusions, care requirements and the claim process are still unconfirmed. Ask for the applicable written terms without inventing their contents. Brands, certifications, specific coating layers and detailed preparation practices are also unconfirmed. Collect those facts and authentic before/after records before making more specific claims. Obtain permission for customer reviews, identifiable photographs and project details. Add project locations, dimensions, dates, prices or outcomes only when documented and approved for use. Do not manufacture reviews, years of experience, certifications, addresses, prices or universal performance guarantees. A clear explanation supported by real business evidence is more useful than calling SMA “best” without substantiation.

[Google Search Console Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)

Track real enquiries, qualified estimates and booked work separately. An email-draft or WhatsApp click is only a contact action; it does not prove a message was sent. Confirm actual received enquiries and ask which page or question helped, without collecting unnecessary personal data. Review pages with visibility but weak clicks for title/intent fit, and pages attracting enquiries for opportunities to add genuine project evidence.

Business Profile work is a later step requiring actual access: verify accurate category, service coverage, contact details and hours, then add approved photographs and genuine reviews. Google's local guidance describes relevance, distance and prominence; publishing a city page does not create a local business location or guarantee map visibility.
